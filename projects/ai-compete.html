Stakeholder engagement · problem formulation

Productive and low-carbon, at the same time

Vinnova · September 2025 – August 2028 · in progress.

AI-COMPETE — Coordinated Multi-Agent AI-Powered Decision Support System for Sustainable Manufacturing — asks how manufacturers can stay productive while cutting their carbon footprint and adapting to volatile energy markets. It is led by the University of Skövde with Uppsala University and industry partners Volvo Penta, Scania, Daloc and Evoma, and its solutions are developed and tested with those partners.

The design constraint that matters most for adoption: the system has to deploy quickly, including at SMEs, without major infrastructure investment.

Architecture · multi-agent AI

Agents that negotiate instead of one objective that hides the trade-off

Three connected areas: coordinated decisions, green scheduling, digital-twin-enabled optimisation.
  • Coordinated decision-making. Several specialised agents, each owning one goal, collaborate through a coordinator to balance objectives that conflict.
  • Green production scheduling. Scheduling methods that tie production activities to renewable energy availability.
  • Digital-twin-enabled optimisation. Digital twins that continuously test and refine production strategies, so a plan is tried on the model before the line runs it.
Production-grade AI stack · in progress

Built to run in a factory, not only in a paper

Agent orchestration, grounded language models and containerised services.
AgentsPython agents orchestrated with Semantic Kernel on Azure AI Foundry.

ServicesFastAPI services in Docker containers, so the system can be deployed without major infrastructure.
