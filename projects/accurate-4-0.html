Stakeholder engagement · problem formulation

Agility on real production lines

KK-stiftelsen · September 2021 – December 2025 · with GKN Aerospace, Volvo Penta and Xylem.

ACCURATE 4.0 — Agile Manufacturing Configuration and Operations in the Era of Industry 4.0 — asked whether optimisation-enabled digital twins, combined with augmented reality and advanced sensing, can move manufacturers toward agile configuration and operation, with the operators’ safety and satisfaction kept in focus. It was the programme my doctoral research sat inside, and its problems came from the partners’ lines: energy-efficient buffer allocation and scheduling on Volvo Penta’s engine line, and buffer allocation in GKN Aerospace’s engine maintenance, repair and overhaul flow, where every unit arrives in a different condition.

Line balancingAllocating tasks and resources to workstations, and sequencing products, with humans and robots working together.
Dynamic bottleneck analysisAnalysing the whole shop-floor flow, finding the bottlenecks as they move, and smoothing production.
User-centred innovationAugmented reality for operators, and reliable collision detection and avoidance.
Live data · descriptive analysis

Connected to the partners’ own data

A cloud-based cyber-physical system that re-reads production data as it updates.

The models were connected to the partners’ production data sources and re-read them as they updated, so an analysis reflected the line as it was running rather than a spreadsheet exported months earlier. The framework for doing this through cloud-based cyber-physical systems is one of the published outputs below.

Diagnostic analysis

Bottlenecks move, so the analysis has to move with them

Dynamic throughput bottleneck analysis.

A bottleneck found in last month’s data may not be the one limiting the line today. Dynamic bottleneck analysis identifies the constraint as the flow changes, which is what makes the improvement decisions after it worth taking. The review that framed this work, The impact of Industry 4.0 on bottleneck analysis in production and manufacturing (Computers & Industrial Engineering, 2022), has passed 100 citations.

Predictive modelling · AI

Simulation, surrogates and meta-learning

Python prototypes: data-driven simulation, and models that learn across design problems.

Data-driven discrete-event simulation predicts how a configuration will perform before it is built or changed. Where simulation is too slow to search, predictive surrogate models stand in for it — and with life-long meta-learning they carry what they learned on earlier design problems into the next one, so each new unreliable production system does not start the learning from zero.

Prescriptive optimisation

From a validated model to a decision on the line

Resource allocation, sequencing and line design as multi-objective problems.
  • Resource allocation through a data-driven, simulation-based decision support system.
  • Production sequencing in hybrid flow shops through simulation-based, knowledge-driven optimisation.
  • Unreliable production system design through predictive model-based multi-objective optimisation.
  • Line balancing with human-robot collaboration through genetic algorithms, and platform-thinking policy through system dynamics with multi-objective optimisation.

Prototyped in Python: SimPy / Salabim simulation, pymoo / DEAP (NSGA-II) optimisation, scikit-learn surrogates, system dynamics, and the programme’s digital twin, cloud CPS and AR work.
