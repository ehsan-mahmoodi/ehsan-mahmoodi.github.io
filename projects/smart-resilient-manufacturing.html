Stakeholder engagement · problem formulation

How does a factory keep running when the ground moves?

KK-stiftelsen Synergy Programme · December 2025 – November 2029 · in progress.

Energy prices rise, machines break down, labour conditions change suddenly. SMART — Resilience-Driven Manufacturing: Developing Adaptive Solutions for Sustainable and Robust Production — asks how Swedish factories can absorb those shocks while staying sustainable and centred on people, in the spirit of Industry 5.0. It is led by the University of Skövde with ABB, Volvo Penta, Daloc, Xylem and IDC West Sweden, and every solution is developed and tested with those partners, on their production problems.

A resilient factory is not one that never gets hit. It is one whose output dips shallowly and recovers fast — so resilience is measured as the depth of the dip and the speed of recovery, not only as uptime.

Three strands · predictive and prescriptive

Operational, energy and socio-technical resilience

Three connected work areas feeding one platform.
  • Operational resilience. Predictive models, simulation and planning tools that let a line prepare for equipment failures and demand changes, and recover quickly when they happen.
  • Energy resilience. Scheduling that follows real-time energy prices and renewable availability, cutting operating cost and environmental impact together.
  • Socio-technical resilience. Adaptive safety systems built on sensors and machine learning, so people and robots can share tasks flexibly without accidents.
My part · multi-agent decision support

Agents that negotiate the schedule

Multi-objective decisions made by cooperating AI agents, checked against simulation.

My work builds the decision layer as multi-agent AI: agents that each own an objective — throughput, energy cost, recovery after a disruption — and negotiate a production schedule, with discrete-event simulation to test a decision before the line commits to it, and multi-objective optimisation to expose the trade-offs rather than hide them in one number.

Related publication: Energy-aware batch scheduling with flexible maintenance under spot electricity pricing and onsite renewable generation, Applied Energy (2026) — the energy-resilience question in its optimisation form. See publications.
